# Third-party data

## Dictionary — `data/dictionary/jmdict-common.json`

Derived from **JMdict**, compiled by the Electronic Dictionary Research and
Development Group (EDRDG) at Monash University
(https://www.edrdg.org/jmdict/j_jmdict.html), from the source file at
http://ftp.edrdg.org/pub/Nihongo/JMdict_e.gz.

> The original JMdict/EDICT files are the property of the Electronic
> Dictionary Research and Development Group, and are used in conformance
> with the Group's licence.

JMdict is made available under a Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike
Licence (CC BY-SA 4.0) — see https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html.
`jmdict-common.json` is a filtered extract (common-word entries only, glosses
trimmed, regenerated by `scripts/build-dictionary.mjs`). Some of its glosses
are copied into `public/feed/content.json` by `scripts/build-feed.mjs` for
tap-to-see meanings. Per the Share-Alike term, both are redistributed under
that same CC BY-SA 4.0 licence. The app credits JMdict on its progress page
(`/words`).

## JLPT levels — `data/dictionary/jlpt-levels.json`

Built by `scripts/build-jlpt-levels.mjs` from
[elzup/jlpt-word-list](https://github.com/elzup/jlpt-word-list) (MIT).

## Tokenizer — kuromoji.js

[kuromoji.js](https://github.com/takuyaa/kuromoji.js) (Apache License 2.0)
with its bundled IPADIC dictionary runs at build time only, in
`scripts/build-feed.mjs`. It isn't shipped to the browser.

## Lessons — `content/`

The words, example sentences, grammar notes, facts, and stories are
original to this project.
