# Third-party data

## Dictionary — `public/dictionary/jmdict-common.json`

Derived from **JMdict**, compiled by the Electronic Dictionary Research and
Development Group (EDRDG) at Monash University
(https://www.edrdg.org/jmdict/j_jmdict.html), from the source file at
http://ftp.edrdg.org/pub/Nihongo/JMdict_e.gz.

> The original JMdict/EDICT files are the property of the Electronic
> Dictionary Research and Development Group, and are used in conformance
> with the Group's licence.

JMdict is made available under a Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike
Licence (CC BY-SA 4.0) — see https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html.
`jmdict-common.json` is a filtered extract (common-word entries only, glosses
trimmed, regenerated by `scripts/build-dictionary.mjs`) and, per the
Share-Alike term, is redistributed under that same CC BY-SA 4.0 licence.

## Classics library — Aozora Bunko

Text fetched at runtime (see `src/lib/aozora.ts`) from
https://aozorahack.org/aozorabunko_text, a mirror of
https://www.aozora.gr.jp — Japan's public-domain digital library. Usage
terms: https://www.aozora.gr.jp/guide/kijyunn.html.

## PDF import — pdf.js

PDF import uses [pdf.js](https://github.com/mozilla/pdf.js) (`pdfjs-dist`),
© Mozilla, Apache License 2.0, loaded only when a PDF is imported.

## Translation model — Xenova/opus-mt-ja-en

The sentence translation toggle (see `src/lib/translate.ts`) lazily loads
the `Xenova/opus-mt-ja-en` model — an ONNX conversion, for in-browser use
with [transformers.js](https://github.com/huggingface/transformers.js), of
`Helsinki-NLP/opus-mt-ja-en` (Language Technology Research Group at the
University of Helsinki), trained on OPUS data with the Marian NMT
framework. Licensed Apache License 2.0. Runs entirely client-side; nothing
is sent to a server.

## JLPT study data — `public/jlpt/*.json`

Built by `scripts/build-jlpt-study-data.mjs` from
[OpenJLPT](https://github.com/evanclan/OpenJLPT) (CC BY-SA 4.0): words, kanji
and grammar points for N5–N1 with example sentences. OpenJLPT in turn uses
Jonathan Waller's JLPT lists (https://www.tanos.co.uk/jlpt/, CC BY) for the
level assignments, JMdict and KANJIDIC2 from the EDRDG (CC BY-SA 4.0), and
example sentences from [Tatoeba](https://tatoeba.org) (CC BY 2.0 FR; each
sentence is credited to its contributors on tatoeba.org). The files are
redistributed under CC BY-SA 4.0. JLPT levels are community consensus, not an
official list: the JLPT publishes none.
